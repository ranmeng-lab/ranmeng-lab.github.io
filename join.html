Opportunities

Join the Meng Lab

We are actively recruiting motivated, creative individuals to join us at the University at Albany, SUNY. We welcome diverse backgrounds, from computational biology and bioinformatics to biomedical science and public health.

Ph.D. Students

We are seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students interested in computational genomics, AI for biomedical imaging, and infectious disease biology. Students can apply through University at Albany graduate programs in Biomedical Sciences, Bioinformatics, and related disciplines.

Strong quantitative background preferred
Experience with Python or R required
Curiosity and enthusiasm required

Postdoctoral Fellows

We seek postdoctoral candidates with expertise in bioinformatics, machine learning, spatial transcriptomics, single-cell genomics, or infectious disease biology. Strong programming skills (Python and/or R) and experience with large-scale genomic datasets are expected.

Ph.D. in relevant field
Track record of peer-reviewed publications
Interest in collaborative, interdisciplinary work

Undergraduate Researchers

Motivated undergraduate students eager to gain hands-on research experience in computational biology, genomics, and biomedical data analysis are welcome to reach out. We provide mentored research opportunities aligned with ongoing lab projects.

Commitment of at least one semester
Basic programming experience helpful
Genuine interest in science

Visiting Scholars

We welcome visiting scholars, faculty, and international researchers interested in short- or long-term collaborations. Visiting scholars will engage with active lab projects and may co-develop new directions in computational genomics, biomedical imaging, or infectious disease biology.

Established research background
Funding or sponsorship confirmed
Proposed duration and research plan

Inclusive & Diverse

We are committed to mentoring students from diverse backgrounds and fostering a welcoming, supportive environment for all.

Interdisciplinary

We work at the interface of AI, biomedical science, and public health, training future leaders who can bridge multiple domains.

Mentorship-Driven

Individual mentorship plans, regular one-on-ones, and a collaborative lab culture help every member thrive and grow.

Ready to Apply?

Please send your CV, a brief statement of research interests (1–2 pages, written by yourself, no generative AI), and contact information for three references to Dr. Ran Meng. We look forward to hearing from you!
