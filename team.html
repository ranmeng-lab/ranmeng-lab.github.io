Dr. Ran Meng

Principal Investigator

Assistant Professor · Department of Biomedical Sciences · University at Albany, SUNY

Dr. Ran Meng is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biomedical Sciences at the University at Albany, SUNY. Before joining UAlbany, she completed her postdoctoral training in the Gerstein Lab at Yale University and earned her Ph.D. in Biochemistry from Texas A&M University.

Her research integrates AI, single-cell genomics, spatial transcriptomics, and biomedical imaging to understand how genetic variation shapes tissue architecture and disease, with a focus on brain disorders and infectious diseases.
